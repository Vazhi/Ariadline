# Pilot Study Design

## Study 1 — Claim interpretation

### Material

Create matched passages that contain:

- attestation claims;
- productivity claims;
- negative evidence;
- speaker judgments;
- parser behavior;
- theoretical analyses.

### Conditions

1. original prose;
2. plain edited prose;
3. SLE prose.

### Tasks

Participants identify:

- what the data directly establish;
- what remains an inference;
- the scope of the claim;
- the stated limitation.

### Primary outcome

Accuracy of claim interpretation.

## Study 2 — Referential ambiguity

Use passages with pronouns, coordination, negation, and comparison ambiguity.

Measure:

- selected interpretation;
- confidence;
- reading time;
- need to reread.

## Study 3 — Annotation instructions

Participants apply uncontrolled and SLE instructions to the same item set.

Measure:

- annotation accuracy;
- inter-annotator agreement;
- completion time;
- questions raised.

## Study 4 — Authoring cost

Writers revise a short methods or grammar section under each style condition.

Measure:

- writing time;
- number of preserved claims;
- violations;
- perceived burden;
- post-task preference.

## Sampling policy

The pilot should not claim universal effectiveness from one institution or subfield. Report participant background, English use, subfield, and prior controlled-language experience.

## Analysis plan

Predefine:

- primary outcomes;
- exclusion rules;
- minimum sample;
- statistical model;
- equivalence or non-inferiority margin for precision preservation;
- correction for multiple secondary outcomes.

## Qualitative review

Collect comments about:

- rules that feel unnatural;
- terms that lose distinctions;
- cases where short sentences damage cohesion;
- cases where repetition improves or harms readability.

## SLE-GE Canto-span test run

The grammar-engineering pilot is governed by [[SLE-GE Canto-span Pilot Baseline v0.1]]. Its material inventory and reader questions are fixed before paired rewriting. Blockers, workarounds, and deviations from the proposed SLE infrastructure are study results and must be included in the authoring-burden analysis.
