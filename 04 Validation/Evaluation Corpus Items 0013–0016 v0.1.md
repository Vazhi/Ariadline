### Authorized meaning brief

> Particle P marks the current discourse topic in the lesson's examples and can support contrast in some contexts. Topic change is one encounter context, not a complete usage rule.

### Uncontrolled draft

## SLE-EVAL-0014 — Phonetic measurement procedure

- **Meaning brief:** `SLE-BRIEF-0014`
- **Brief authority:** project-constructed evaluation specification; no external source-author authority
- **Provenance:** project-constructed fictional editorial test item
- **Domain:** phonetics
- **Method:** acoustic measurement
- **Illustrative context:** Illustrative Language M
- **Framework prompt:** laboratory phonetics
- **Genre:** procedure document
- **Pattern:** `SLE-PATTERN-0011`
- **Rules tested:** `SLE-RULE-0004`, `SLE-RULE-0005`, `SLE-RULE-0008`, `SLE-RULE-0013`, `SLE-RULE-0016`, `SLE-RULE-0017`, `SLE-RULE-0022`

### Authorized meaning brief

> For eligible stop tokens, measure from release burst to periodic voicing. Exclude only tokens whose landmarks cannot be identified under stated criteria. Calculate a speaker mean from remaining eligible tokens.

### Uncontrolled draft

> Measure VOT at the burst, exclude bad tokens, and average the rest for every speaker.

### Proposed controlled alternative

> For each eligible stop token, mark the release burst and the onset of periodic voicing. Calculate voice onset time as the interval between those landmarks. Exclude a token only when one landmark cannot be identified under the stated acoustic criteria. Then calculate the speaker-level mean from the remaining eligible tokens.

### Pair audit

- **Length:** 15 → 50 words (+35); 1 → 4 sentences (+3)
- **Structure and terminology:** Defines landmarks, eligibility, exclusion condition, sequence, and aggregation.
- **Uncontrolled-to-brief result:** ambiguous relative to brief
- **Controlled-to-brief internal result:** matches brief
- **Literal passage-to-passage equivalence:** not equivalent by design when the uncontrolled draft overstates or obscures the authorized brief
- **Independent preservation result:** not determined
- **Risk:** The fictional protocol does not settle prevoicing, multiple bursts, or automated landmark choices.
