# Ariadline Open-License Material Candidate Package v0.1

## Purpose

This package supports human issue #42 by reducing the first-pass discovery burden for authentic linguistic materials. It records official source and license metadata, possible coverage value, and the human checks that remain necessary.

It does not select passages or create authorized materials.

## Workflow

1. A human reviews the candidate pool and rejects unsuitable sources.
2. A human confirms the exact work, version, license notice, and any third-party exclusions. A publisher or rights holder may confirm publication and rights information but is not automatically an authority on the author's intended meaning.
3. A human proposes an exact passage boundary and necessary surrounding context without placing restricted text in the public repository.
4. A legitimate author, translator, source, or community meaning authority approves the meaning record and question route.
5. Permission, participant exposure, quotation, redistribution, access, community, and privacy states are recorded separately.
6. Only then may a candidate enter P/S condition development under human issue #44, using the condition templates from issue #43 or their successors.

## Status vocabulary

Every entry in this package is `candidate—not approved`.

This status means only that an official page appears to offer enough stable metadata for human screening. It does not mean:

- that every part of the work is covered by the displayed license;
- that embedded examples, images, audio, transcripts, maps, or source datasets may be reused;
- that the author or community has approved editing or participant exposure;
- that the intended meaning can be established without direct authority;
- that the source fits the final study design;
- that an open license resolves privacy, ethics, cultural authority, accessibility, or research-governance questions.

## Public-repository boundary

Do not copy candidate passages into this repository. Store only citation, locator, license, screening state, and repository-safe summaries until the approved access plan and authority route permit more.
