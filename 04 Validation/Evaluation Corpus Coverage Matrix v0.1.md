# Evaluation Corpus Coverage Matrix v0.1

## Purpose

This register describes the internal-audit coverage and representativeness limits of [[Multi-Domain Ariadline Evaluation Corpus v0.1]] and the separately bounded [[Canto-span Evaluation Subset v0.1]].

The corpus is test material, not normative evidence. Its anonymized contexts are prompts for editorial evaluation, not facts about languages, communities, theories, or methods.

## Source and authority distribution

- independent project-constructed items: 16 of 18;
- Canto-span stress-test items: 2 of 18;
- authentic permission-compatible external excerpts: 0;
- source-author-contributed passages: 0;
- community-governed passages: 0;
- non-English-original passages: 0;
- translated passages with translator review: 0.

Canto-span supplies 11.1% of the items and cannot satisfy any independent coverage requirement.

## What v0.1 covers

The independent set contains distinct constructed prompts for:

- 16 domains or document purposes;
- corpus, theoretical, sequential, comparative, elicitation, experimental, lexicographic, documentation, annotation, computational, pedagogical, phonetic, and governance methods;
- framework-neutral, formal, functional, conversation-analytic, variationist, typological, descriptive, annotation, computational, and cross-framework perspectives;
- 13 of the 14 proposed document patterns directly.

This is **prompt coverage for internal rule auditing**. It is not authentic domain, theory, method, or language representation.

## Language-coverage boundary

The anonymized labels `Language A` through `Language M` prevent fictional details from being mistaken for real-language claims.

They do not establish multilingual coverage. Authentic language coverage remains zero because:

- every passage is written originally in English;
- no item contains independently reviewed object-language material;
- no translation has translator or source-author review;
- no community authority controls an item;
- signed-language material is represented only by English resource prose.

Issue #6 therefore closes only the first internal constructed-corpus stage. Authentic multilingual evaluation remains a required later gate.

## Rule and pattern auditability

Every item records:

- a stable item ID;
- a stable meaning brief ID;
- full `SLE-RULE-*` identifiers;
- complete `SLE-PATTERN-*` identifiers;
- exact word and sentence counts and deltas;
- structure and terminology changes;
- uncontrolled-to-brief and controlled-to-brief results;
- literal pair relation;
- independent preservation as `not determined`.

Direct pattern coverage:

- represented independently: `SLE-PATTERN-0001` through `SLE-PATTERN-0012` and `SLE-PATTERN-0014`;
- not represented independently: `SLE-PATTERN-0013`;
- represented only in the bounded Canto-span subset: `SLE-PATTERN-0013`.

Direct interlinear-glossing evaluation is absent.

## Overrepresentation risks

1. All independent items are constructed by the same project.
2. All passages use English metalanguage.
3. Every item was designed around the current 24 proposed rules.
4. Short research and documentation passages dominate.
5. Controlled alternatives frequently add explicit counts and limitations.
6. No failed controlled alternative is retained in v0.1.
7. No full document or mixed-pattern document is tested.
8. No authentic community, author, translator, or publisher authority is represented.

## Required later additions

Before effectiveness, representativeness, or stabilization claims, add:

- authentic permission-compatible passages from independent authors;
- non-English-original passages;
- independently reviewed translations;
- community-authored and community-controlled documentation;
- video-aware signed-language materials;
- full-document and mixed-pattern samples;
- rejected and disputed controlled alternatives;
- direct interlinear-glossing tests;
- an independent limitation and open-question record;
- additional historical, morphological, phonological, acquisition, psycholinguistic, accessibility, peer-review, and public-facing genres.

## Representativeness gate

Before any claim that Ariadline works across linguistics:

1. at least half of evaluated passages must come from independent, permission-compatible, non-Canto-span sources;
2. no repository, institution, language, theory, method, or author group may supply most evaluated passages;
3. non-English-original and translated material must receive language-specific meaning-preservation review;
4. community-controlled material must preserve access and authority boundaries;
5. full-document tests must supplement short-passage pairs;
6. unresolved coverage gaps must remain visible.

## Disposition

The matrix supports v0.1 as an internal constructed audit corpus. It does not authorize effectiveness, authentic multilingual coverage, or stabilization claims.
