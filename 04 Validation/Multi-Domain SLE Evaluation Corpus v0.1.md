# Multi-Domain SLE Evaluation Corpus v0.1

## Status and authority

This corpus is project-constructed internal audit material for the proposed SLE for Linguistics reference artifact.

It is not authentic multilingual evidence, source-author prose, normative justification, or proof that a controlled alternative is better. The illustrative contexts are anonymized so fictional claims cannot be mistaken for facts about real languages or communities.

Each item declares a stable authorized meaning brief before the uncontrolled and controlled passages. Both passages are checked against that brief.

The uncontrolled passage is a deliberately defective draft. It can overstate, underspecify, or obscure the authorized meaning. Literal passage-to-passage equivalence is therefore not expected and must not be claimed.

The controlled alternative can enter later testing only when it matches the authorized brief in project-internal review. Independent preservation remains `not determined` until an independent reviewer checks the item.

## Mechanical count convention

The v0.1 pair audits use one reproducible editorial count convention:

- a word is a Unicode letter-or-number run;
- an internal apostrophe or hyphen remains part of the word when surrounded by word characters;
- punctuation otherwise separates words;
- a comma-grouped number such as `1,100` is counted as two numeric runs under this mechanical convention;
- a sentence is counted at a displayed terminal period, question mark, or exclamation mark;
- headings, labels, and audit notes are excluded from passage counts.

These counts describe surface change only. They do not establish readability, cohesion, or improvement.

## Interpretation rule

The internal result `matches brief` means only that a project-constructed controlled alternative is consistent with its project-constructed authorized brief.

It does not establish literal equivalence to the uncontrolled draft, source-author meaning preservation, correctness of fictional linguistic content, improved comprehension, authentic domain or language coverage, or suitability for stabilization.

Every independent preservation result remains `not determined`.

## Use restrictions

- Do not cite fictional data as linguistic evidence.
- Do not call illustrative contexts authentic language coverage.
- Do not describe a controlled alternative as superior before evaluation.
- Do not infer rule validity from a constructed pair.
- Do not use the Canto-span subset to define SLE.
- Do not require a machine-readable corpus.
