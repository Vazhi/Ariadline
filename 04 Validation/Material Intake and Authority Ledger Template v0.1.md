## Automation-safe export

A machine-readable export may contain stable IDs, broad metadata, lifecycle states, and non-identifying status fields.

Do not place names, contact details, consent forms, exact institutions, exact locations, restricted text, unpublished drafts, or rare identifying combinations in a public or synthetic fixture.
