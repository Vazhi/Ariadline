## Central question

Does a 12-rule Ariadline candidate core add important value beyond competent ordinary expert editing without increasing meaning loss, burden, unnaturalness, repetition, fragmentation, disagreement, or bias?

## Scope

The package:

- narrows evaluation without changing the 24-rule proposed draft;
- keeps stable `SLE-RULE-*` identifiers after the Ariadline rename;
- treats S-versus-P as the publication-relevant comparison;
- requires authentic authorized passages and independent preservation review;
- permits rule removal, project reconception, project stop, and insufficient evidence.

## Boundary

This is planning material only. It does not approve human research, acquire passages, grant permissions, certify meaning, recruit participants, freeze statistics, preregister a study, or change Ariadline’s normative status.
