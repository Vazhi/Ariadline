# Ariadline Kill-Test Execution Packet v0.1

## Purpose

This is the operational front door for issue #53. It translates [[Ariadline Minimal Kill-Test Package v0.1]] into compact forms that humans can approve and use.

## Packet contents

- [[Ariadline Kill-Test Passage Intake Form v0.1]]
- [[Ariadline Matched Editor Briefs v0.1]]
- [[Ariadline Preservation Review Form v0.1]]
- [[Ariadline Reader Task and Scoring Packet v0.1]]
- [[Ariadline Kill-Test Execution Checklist v0.1]]
- [[Ariadline Kill-Test Execution Packet Validation v0.1]]

## Workflow

1. Screen candidate passages with the intake form.
2. Human authorities approve use and meaning records.
3. Freeze identical shared information for P and S.
4. Produce matched P and S edits.
5. Conduct masked independent preservation review.
6. Exclude or retain adverse pairs according to the frozen protocol.
7. Freeze passage-specific reader tasks and scoring keys.
8. Complete launch and masking checks.
9. Run only after human approval and preregistration.
10. Apply rule-level and project-level decision routes.

## Compatibility

Visible project language uses Ariadline. Stable `SLE-RULE-*` identifiers and U/P/S condition codes remain unchanged for traceability and compatibility with existing evaluation records.

## Authority boundary

The packet supplies forms only. It does not select authentic passages, approve use, define legitimate meaning, certify preservation, authorize recruitment, preregister a study, or decide whether Ariadline continues.
