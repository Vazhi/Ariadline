# Ariadline Kill-Test Execution Packet v0.1

## Purpose

This is the operational front door for issue #53. It translates [[Ariadline Minimal Kill-Test Package v0.1]] into compact forms that humans can approve and use.

- [[Ariadline Kill-Test Execution Packet Validation v0.1]]

## Workflow

1. Screen candidate passages and retain rejected or reserve records.
2. Human authorities approve use and authorized meaning records.
3. Freeze one identical rule-neutral communication-risk packet for P and S.
4. Preregister candidate-rule applicability as restricted administrative metadata; do not show it to either editor.
5. Derive and freeze passage-specific task constructs and scoring keys from the source, meaning record, and neutral risk before condition outputs are examined.
6. Assign different editors to P and S for each passage and activate contamination controls.
7. Produce matched P and S edits; the S editor determines and logs applicability independently.
8. Audit comparability and immutable output versions.
9. Conduct masked independent preservation review using deterministic aggregation rules.
10. Exclude failed or unresolved conditions and pairs from benefit analysis while retaining adverse results.
11. Build reader- and scorer-facing packets without restricted rule, condition, editor, or action-log metadata.
12. Complete launch, masking, accessibility, assignment, withdrawal, deviation, statistical, and preregistration checks.
13. Run only after all required human approvals and registration gates pass.
14. Apply rule-level and project-level decision routes without hiding adverse or insufficient-evidence outcomes.

## Information separation

- **Shared editor packet:** source, authorized meaning, purpose, readership, neutral communication risk, constraints, resources, and timing.
- **S-only process guidance:** the 12-rule candidate core, without the coordinator’s preregistered applicability mapping.
- **Restricted administration:** rule mappings, condition identities, editor identities, action logs, and code keys.
- **Reader packet:** masked text and registered questions only.
- **Scorer packet:** masked responses and frozen scoring key only.

## Compatibility

Visible project language uses Ariadline. Stable `SLE-RULE-*` identifiers and U/P/S condition codes remain unchanged for traceability and compatibility with existing evaluation records.

## Authority boundary

The packet supplies forms only. It does not select authentic passages, approve use, define legitimate meaning, certify preservation, authorize recruitment, preregister a study, or decide whether Ariadline continues.
