## Workflow

1. Screen candidate passages and retain rejected or reserve records.
2. Human authorities approve lawful use and authorized meaning records. Rights and publication authority remain separate from meaning authority.
3. Freeze one identical rule-neutral communication-risk packet for P and S.
4. Register candidate-rule applicability as restricted administrative metadata; do not show it to either editor.
5. Derive and freeze passage-specific task constructs and scoring keys from the source, meaning record, and neutral risk before condition outputs are examined.
6. Assign different editors to P and S for each passage and activate contamination controls.
7. Produce matched P and S edits; the S editor determines and logs applicability independently.
8. Audit comparability and immutable output versions.
9. Conduct masked independent preservation review using deterministic aggregation rules.
10. Exclude failed or unresolved conditions and pairs from benefit analysis while retaining adverse results or permitted tombstones.
11. Create the frozen participant assignment schedule and hash under the approved counterbalancing plan.
12. Build reader- and scorer-facing packets without restricted rule, condition, editor, or action-log metadata.
13. Freeze mechanical exclusions, data separation, retention, deviation, unmasking, and analysis rules.
14. Review the synthetic dry-run defects and repaired controls against the authentic implementation.
15. Complete launch, masking, accessibility, assignment, withdrawal, oversight, and registration checks.
16. Run only after all required human approvals and registration gates pass.
17. Apply rule-level and project-level decision routes without hiding adverse or insufficient-evidence outcomes.

## Information separation

- **Shared editor packet:** source, authorized meaning, purpose, readership, neutral communication risk, constraints, resources, and timing.
- **S-only process guidance:** only the human-approved candidate core, without the coordinator's registered applicability mapping.
- **Restricted materials and authority:** passages, permissions, authorized meaning records, authority contacts, and access restrictions.
- **Restricted administration:** condition mappings, editor identities, rule mappings, action logs, assignment keys, randomization keys, and unmasking keys.
- **Reader packet:** masked text and registered questions only.
- **Scorer packet:** masked responses and frozen scoring key only.
- **Analysis record:** minimized de-identified data linked through random IDs after the authorized unmasking sequence.

## Compatibility

Visible project language uses Ariadline. Stable `SLE-RULE-*` identifiers and U/P/S condition codes remain unchanged for traceability and compatibility with existing evaluation records.

## Authority boundary

The packet and synthetic dry run supply planning documents, forms, and procedure evidence only. They do not approve the core, select authentic passages, grant permission, define legitimate meaning, certify authentic preservation, approve oversight, authorize recruitment, freeze the final statistics, preregister a study, analyze participant evidence, or decide whether Ariadline continues.
