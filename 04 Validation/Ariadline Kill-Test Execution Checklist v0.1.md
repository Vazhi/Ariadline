# Ariadline Kill-Test Execution Checklist v0.1

Every launch item requires a human owner, date, evidence locator, and result: `pass`, `fail`, `not applicable`, or `not determined`.

A launch result can be `ready` only when every applicable required item is `pass`. Every `not applicable` result requires a recorded rationale. Any required `fail` or `not determined` result blocks launch.

## A. Scope, governance, and materials

- [ ] Human issue #40 approved the exact 8–12-rule candidate core and project framing.
- [ ] Oversight, consent, privacy, retention, accessibility, compensation, incident, and withdrawal routes are approved under issue #46.
- [ ] Passage permissions and access restrictions are approved under issue #42.
- [ ] Legitimate meaning authority is recorded for every passage; publisher or rights authority is not treated automatically as meaning authority.
- [ ] Ten to twelve eligible passages are available.
- [ ] At least three independent subfield, method, or practice groups are represented.
- [ ] No source, venue, theory, language, project, contributor network, or subfield dominates the set.
- [ ] Canto-span does not satisfy independent coverage.
- [ ] Rejected and reserve passages are retained with reasons.
- [ ] Passage selection was not based on expected P-versus-S performance.

## B. Shared information and conditions

- [ ] P represents competent ordinary expert editing.
- [ ] S applies only the exact human-approved candidate core.
- [ ] P and S receive the same source and meaning-record versions.
- [ ] P and S receive the same frozen rule-neutral communication-risk brief.
- [ ] The shared brief contains no rule IDs, Ariadline terminology, expected direction, or condition-specific diagnosis.
- [ ] P and S receive the same substantive information, constraints, timing, and allowed resources.
- [ ] S differs only by receiving the approved candidate-core guidance.
- [ ] The coordinator's registered applicability mapping is held from both editors.
- [ ] The S editor records an independent applicability judgment during editing.
- [ ] U is absent unless separately authorized and registered.
- [ ] Editor qualification and matching are recorded.
- [ ] Editing time and resource use are recorded.

## C. Editor assignment and contamination

- [ ] Different editors produce P and S for the same passage or meaning record.
- [ ] Editors cannot access the other condition's output, logs, questions, preservation record, or participant result.
- [ ] Editors working across conditions on different passages have registered counterbalancing and order controls.
- [ ] Prior Ariadline exposure and contamination risks are recorded.
- [ ] Any prohibited access or same-record carryover triggers comparability review.

## D. Preservation

- [ ] P and S conditions have independent masked preservation review under issue #44.
- [ ] Reviewers receive no condition label, rule ID, editor identity, action log, expected direction, or other-condition output.
- [ ] Edited text versions and hashes are recorded.
- [ ] Required meaning-authority confirmation is complete.
- [ ] Any critical or major non-preservation produces overall `not preserved`.
- [ ] Any unresolved material dimension produces `not determined` unless the result is already `not preserved`.
- [ ] Overall `preserved` requires every applicable material dimension to be preserved.
- [ ] Minor and editorial differences are explicitly confirmed nonmaterial.
- [ ] Disagreement and dissent are retained.
- [ ] Adjudication rules are frozen.
- [ ] A failed or unresolved condition is excluded from benefit analysis.
- [ ] A P/S pair with either condition ineligible is excluded from the primary benefit comparison.
- [ ] Adverse preservation results remain reported.

## E. Tasks and scoring

- [ ] Each passage has a registered authentic rule-neutral communication risk.
- [ ] Only relevant reader tasks are used.
- [ ] No more than three primary outcomes are frozen.
- [ ] Task constructs and scoring keys were derived only from source, authorized meaning, neutral risk, construct, and accessibility requirements.
- [ ] Task designers and scorers did not inspect condition outputs, editor logs, condition mappings, or participant outcomes before freeze.
- [ ] Every scored question has a frozen question ID, meaning-record link, accepted elements, prohibited unsupported elements, and adjudication rule.
- [ ] Pass, fail, and borderline examples are frozen where useful.
- [ ] Reader-facing packets contain no rule IDs, condition labels, editor metadata, meaning-record text, or scoring answers.
- [ ] Scorer-facing packets contain only masked responses and the frozen scoring key.
- [ ] Rule IDs, condition mappings, editor identities, and action logs remain restricted administrative metadata.
- [ ] Scorer calibration and agreement procedures are frozen.
- [ ] Any masking failure or outcome-visible key change triggers eligibility and deviation review.
- [ ] Meaning-preservation failure cannot be offset by reader scores.
- [ ] Subjective clarity, confidence, or preference is not used as a substitute for scored comprehension.

## F. Data separation and identifiers

- [ ] [[Ariadline Kill-Test Data, Assignment, and Analysis Plan v0.1]] is approved or replaced by a human-approved frozen plan.
- [ ] Contact and consent records, participant key, restricted materials, restricted administrative mappings, de-identified research data, and release records are access-separated.
- [ ] Participant and record IDs are random and do not encode condition, institution, country, language, role, recruitment source, health, or accessibility details.
- [ ] Direct identifiers are absent from assignment, response, score, preservation, and analysis records.
- [ ] Rare combinations and free text have a disclosure-review route.
- [ ] Access roles, encryption, backup, transfer, incident response, retention, deletion, withdrawal limits, and release conditions are approved.
- [ ] Every release will identify protocol, material, condition, assignment, scoring, cleaning, exclusion, deviation, and analysis versions.

## G. Assignment and participant protection

- [ ] Sample target and analysis are approved under issue #46.
- [ ] Recruitment quotas and insider caps are frozen.
- [ ] Most participants are independent of Ariadline and Canto-span development.
- [ ] No participant sees P and S for the same meaning record.
- [ ] P/S exposure is balanced by material and order as closely as the approved design permits.
- [ ] Domain and method exposure is checked against the frozen schedule.
- [ ] The randomization or counterbalancing algorithm, seed, schedule version, and hash are frozen.
- [ ] Replacement and late-assignment rules are frozen and do not use condition-labelled outcomes.
- [ ] Accessibility accommodations are compatible with tasks and are not treated as inferior performance.
- [ ] Withdrawal removes later assignments and follows approved data handling.
- [ ] Participant identifiers remain outside public or analysis records.

## H. Exclusions, deviations, and unmasking

- [ ] Mechanical exclusion codes are frozen before participant exposure.
- [ ] Exclusion rules are limited to approved objective eligibility, consent, duplicate, withdrawal, technical, missingness, masking, assignment, comparability, preservation, and protocol-integrity conditions.
- [ ] No record is excluded because it favors P, criticizes Ariadline, reports burden or bias, selects uncertainty, or supports a null, adverse, revise, stop, or insufficient-evidence result.
- [ ] Unregistered exclusions are recorded as deviations rather than silently treated as planned.
- [ ] Every deviation records date, phase, affected scope, outcome visibility, reason, corrective action, eligibility effect, interpretive effect, and responsible role.
- [ ] The original frozen protocol remains preserved after amendments.
- [ ] Unmasking sequence, authorized roles, date, scope, and purpose are frozen and logged.

## I. Execution controls

- [ ] Protocol, material, condition, task, scoring, assignment, exclusion, deviation, and analysis versions are immutable before launch.
- [ ] The synthetic dry run under issue #45 has been reviewed and all launch blockers are resolved.
- [ ] Outcome data were not visible during setup changes.
- [ ] Adverse findings trigger the registered pause or stop route.
- [ ] No automation can promote `not determined`, approve preservation, authorize recruitment, or advance study status.
- [ ] An immutable human-approved registration or dated frozen record exists before condition-labelled outcome inspection.

## J. Analysis and disposition

- [ ] The primary comparison is S versus P.
- [ ] Item-level and rule-level results precede package-level conclusions.
- [ ] P successes and S-created problems are retained.
- [ ] Meaning preservation, burden, naturalness, cohesion, repetition, fragmentation, applicability agreement, and bias are reported alongside comprehension.
- [ ] Uncertainty, missingness, scorer disagreement, withdrawals, exclusions, and deviations are visible.
- [ ] Hard safety failures, descriptive warnings, and insufficient evidence are distinguished.
- [ ] No unsupported numerical threshold or universal generalization is introduced.
- [ ] Null, mixed, harmful, revise, stop, and insufficient-evidence outcomes remain available.
- [ ] Automated analysis under issue #47 uses only authorized de-identified data or publishable aggregates.
- [ ] Final action follows [[Ariadline Kill-Test Decision Matrix v0.1]] and human issue #48.
