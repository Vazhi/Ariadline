# Ariadline Kill-Test Execution Checklist v0.1

Every launch item requires a human owner, date, evidence locator, and result: `pass`, `fail`, `not applicable`, or `not determined`.

## A. Governance and materials

- [ ] Oversight, consent, privacy, retention, accessibility, and withdrawal routes are approved.
- [ ] Passage permissions and access restrictions are approved.
- [ ] Legitimate meaning authority is recorded for every passage.
- [ ] Ten to twelve eligible passages are available.
- [ ] At least three independent subfield or practice groups are represented.
- [ ] No source or subfield exceeds half of the set.
- [ ] Canto-span does not satisfy independent coverage.

## B. Conditions

- [ ] P represents competent ordinary expert editing.
- [ ] P and S receive the same source and meaning-record versions.
- [ ] P and S receive the same substantive information and allowed resources.
- [ ] S differs only by candidate-core guidance and applicability decisions.
- [ ] U is absent unless separately authorized and registered.
- [ ] Editor qualification and matching are recorded.
- [ ] Editing time and resource use are recorded.
- [ ] Cross-condition leakage controls are active.

## C. Preservation

- [ ] P and S conditions have independent masked preservation review.
- [ ] Required authority confirmation is complete.
- [ ] Critical and major losses are visible.
- [ ] `Not determined` remains unresolved.
- [ ] Disagreement and dissent are retained.
- [ ] Adjudication rules are frozen.
- [ ] Ineligible condition pairs are excluded before reader exposure.

## D. Tasks and scoring

- [ ] Each passage has a registered authentic communication risk.
- [ ] Only relevant reader tasks are used.
- [ ] No more than three primary outcomes are frozen.
- [ ] Scoring keys include accepted elements and prohibited unsupported inferences.
- [ ] Pass, fail, and borderline examples are frozen.
- [ ] Reader and scorer condition labels are masked.
- [ ] Scorer calibration and agreement procedures are frozen.
- [ ] Meaning-preservation failure cannot be offset by reader scores.

## E. Assignment and participant protection

- [ ] Sample target and analysis are approved and preregistered.
- [ ] Recruitment quotas and insider caps are frozen.
- [ ] No participant sees P and S for the same meaning record.
- [ ] Order and domain balance are checked.
- [ ] Accessibility accommodations are compatible with tasks.
- [ ] Withdrawal removes later assignments and follows approved data handling.
- [ ] Participant identifiers remain outside public or analysis records.

## F. Execution controls

- [ ] Protocol, material, condition, task, scoring, and analysis versions are immutable.
- [ ] Randomization seed and schedule hash are recorded.
- [ ] Outcome data were not visible during setup changes.
- [ ] Deviations have owners, affected scope, corrective action, and interpretive effect.
- [ ] Adverse findings trigger the registered pause or stop route.
- [ ] No automation can promote `not determined`, approve preservation, or advance study status.

## G. Completion and disposition

- [ ] Participant flow and exclusions are reported.
- [ ] Rule-level results are reported before package-level conclusions.
- [ ] P successes and S-created problems are retained.
- [ ] Burden, naturalness, cohesion, repetition, fragmentation, and bias are reported.
- [ ] Null, mixed, harmful, stop, and insufficient-evidence outcomes remain available.
- [ ] Final action follows [[Ariadline Kill-Test Decision Matrix v0.1]] and human governance.
