# Evaluation Corpus Items 0001–0004 v0.1

These project-constructed items belong to [[Multi-Domain SLE Evaluation Corpus v0.1]]. Each authorized meaning brief is declared before the two passages. `Matches brief` is an internal construction check, not independent semantic-equivalence approval.

## SLE-EVAL-0001 — Bounded corpus observation

- **Meaning brief:** `SLE-BRIEF-0001`
- **Brief authority:** project-constructed evaluation specification; no external source-author authority
- **Provenance:** project-constructed fictional editorial test item
- **Domain:** descriptive grammar
- **Method:** small recorded-text corpus
- **Illustrative context:** Illustrative spoken Language A
- **Framework prompt:** framework-neutral description
- **Genre:** grammar section
- **Pattern:** `SLE-PATTERN-0001`
- **Rules tested:** `SLE-RULE-0003`, `SLE-RULE-0009`, `SLE-RULE-0019`, `SLE-RULE-0020`, `SLE-RULE-0021`, `SLE-RULE-0022`, `SLE-RULE-0023`

### Authorized meaning brief

> The fictional sample contains 31 eligible subordinate-clause tokens. Every observed token contains the classifier. The record does not authorize a language-wide necessity claim.

### Uncontrolled draft

> The classifier is obligatory in subordinate clauses, and speakers never omit it.

### Proposed controlled alternative

> In the fictional recorded-text sample, the classifier occurs in all 31 eligible subordinate-clause tokens. The sample contains no omission. This result does not establish that every speaker requires the classifier in every subordinate-clause context.

### Pair audit

- **Length:** 12 → 34 words (+22); 1 → 3 sentences (+2)
- **Structure and terminology:** Replaces obligatory and never with sample-bound observation and limitation.
- **Uncontrolled-to-brief result:** does not match brief
- **Controlled-to-brief internal result:** matches brief
- **Literal passage-to-passage equivalence:** not equivalent by design when the uncontrolled draft overstates or obscures the authorized brief
- **Independent preservation result:** not determined
- **Risk:** A source author who intends a universal grammatical claim must supply independent support rather than use this controlled alternative.

### Authorized meaning brief

### Pair audit

## SLE-EVAL-0003 — Interactional function and distribution

- **Meaning brief:** `SLE-BRIEF-0003`
- **Brief authority:** project-constructed evaluation specification; no external source-author authority
- **Provenance:** project-constructed fictional editorial test item
- **Domain:** discourse and interactional linguistics
- **Method:** sequential analysis of recorded conversation
- **Illustrative context:** Illustrative Language C
- **Framework prompt:** conversation-analytic and functional interpretation
- **Genre:** phenomenon description and analysis
- **Pattern:** `SLE-PATTERN-0002 + SLE-PATTERN-0003`
- **Rules tested:** `SLE-RULE-0003`, `SLE-RULE-0006`, `SLE-RULE-0019`, `SLE-RULE-0020`, `SLE-RULE-0022`, `SLE-RULE-0023`

### Authorized meaning brief

> Discourse marker D occurs at 18 turn transitions in the fictional sample. Eleven following turns display disagreement. Disagreement is recurrent, not exclusive.

### Uncontrolled draft

> Speakers use marker D to manage turns, and it always marks disagreement.

### Proposed controlled alternative

> In the fictional twelve-conversation sample, marker D occurs at 18 turn transitions. In 11 cases, the following turn displays disagreement with the prior speaker. The analysis treats disagreement as one recurrent function in this sample; it does not classify disagreement as the marker's only function.

### Pair audit

- **Length:** 12 → 45 words (+33); 1 → 3 sentences (+2)
- **Structure and terminology:** Replaces always with an explicit distribution and a non-exclusive functional claim.
- **Uncontrolled-to-brief result:** does not match brief

- **Independent preservation result:** not determined

### Authorized meaning brief
