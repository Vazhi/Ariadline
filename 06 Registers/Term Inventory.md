# Term Inventory

| ID | Preferred term or phrase | Status | Controlled meaning | Contrast or warning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SLE-TERM-0001 | attested | proposed | Recorded in an identified source or dataset. | Does not by itself mean productive, frequent, or acceptable. |
| SLE-TERM-0002 | acceptable | proposed | Receives a defined positive judgment under a stated task and population. | Do not treat as a context-free property without evidence. |
| SLE-TERM-0003 | grammatical | proposed | Conforms to a declared grammatical analysis or speaker-competence claim. | Define the intended theoretical and evidential use. |
| SLE-TERM-0004 | productive | proposed | Extends to novel eligible items or contexts under stated conditions. | Attestation alone is insufficient. |
| SLE-TERM-0005 | frequent | proposed | Meets a stated frequency measure in a stated dataset. | Do not use without a comparison or threshold. |
| SLE-TERM-0006 | corpus token | proposed | One occurrence identified by the corpus tokenization policy. | Distinguish token from type. |
| SLE-TERM-0007 | constructed example | proposed | An example created or materially modified by the author. | Not a corpus attestation. |
| SLE-TERM-0008 | observation | proposed | A direct recorded result under a stated method. | Separate from interpretation. |
| SLE-TERM-0009 | analysis | proposed | An interpretation of data under stated assumptions or a framework. | Not identical to the data. |
| SLE-TERM-0010 | hypothesis | proposed | A provisional claim that makes testable predictions. | State possible falsifiers. |
| SLE-TERM-0011 | shows | proposed | Evidence directly establishes the stated result within the declared method and scope. | Use more cautiously than *supports*. |
| SLE-TERM-0012 | supports | proposed | Evidence increases confidence but is not sufficient alone. | Does not mean proves. |
| SLE-TERM-0013 | suggests | proposed | Evidence provides a preliminary reason to consider a claim. | Weak evidence force. |
| SLE-TERM-0014 | is consistent with | proposed | Evidence does not contradict a claim, while alternatives remain. | Very weak positive force. |
| SLE-TERM-0015 | does not establish | proposed | Available evidence is insufficient for the stated conclusion. | Not necessarily evidence against the conclusion. |
| SLE-TERM-0016 | must | proposed | Conformance requirement. | Not prediction or strong recommendation. |
| SLE-TERM-0017 | should | proposed | Recommendation with possible justified exceptions. | Not requirement. |
| SLE-TERM-0018 | may | proposed | Permission. | Do not use for capability. |
| SLE-TERM-0019 | can | proposed | Capability or factual possibility. | Do not use for permission in normative text. |
| SLE-TERM-0020 | variety | proposed | A defined language variety selected for the study or description. | Avoid unqualified dialect-language assumptions. |
| SLE-TERM-0021 | register | proposed | A language-use pattern associated with a defined situation or activity. | State operational criteria. |
| SLE-TERM-0022 | speaker judgment | proposed | A response collected through a stated elicitation task. | Not identical to spontaneous usage. |
| SLE-TERM-0023 | negative evidence | proposed | Failure to observe an expected result within a defined search or test. | State sensitivity and search space. |
| SLE-TERM-0024 | system behavior | proposed | Output or action of a specified software system and configuration. | Not automatically a language fact. |

See [[Terminology Control]] and use [[Ariadline Term Entry Template]] for full records.
