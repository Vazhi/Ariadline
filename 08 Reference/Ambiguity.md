# Ambiguity

Ambiguity exists when an expression permits more than one materially different interpretation in context.

Ariadline prioritizes ambiguities that affect:

- the claim;
- the evidence;
- scope;
- polarity;
- quantification;
- antecedents;
- analytical category.

See [[Ambiguity and Referential Clarity]].
