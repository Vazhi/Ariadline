# Attestation and Productivity

## Attestation

A form is **attested** when it is recorded in an identified source or dataset.

## Productivity

A pattern is **productive** when it can extend to eligible novel items or contexts under defined conditions.

Attestation is evidence that a form occurs in the identified data. Attestation alone does not establish unrestricted productivity, frequency, acceptability, or grammatical status.
