# Glossary

## Authority

This glossary is an informative terminology guide for [[Ariadline Reference Artifact v0.1 Draft]]. It summarizes current package usage. It does not create an Ariadline obligation or a universal linguistic definition.

The narrower controlling rule, pattern, profile, conformance, and governance modules determine normative meaning. A project extension may define additional or different local terms when it identifies their scope and does not present them as universal Ariadline terminology.

## Ariadline

A proposed controlled form of English for clear linguistic description, evidence reporting, research documentation, editorial review, and related technical communication.

## Controlled natural language

A natural language used with deliberate restrictions or conventions to improve interpretation, consistency, translation, review, or bounded machine assistance.

## Normative

Creating or controlling an obligation, prohibition, recommendation, permission, capability distinction, conformance rule, profile mapping, or governance requirement within the stated Ariadline version.

## Informative

Supporting understanding or application without creating an Ariadline obligation.

## Normative verbal form

A word or phrase assigned a declared function such as requirement, prohibition, recommendation, permission, or capability. Evidence wording is not a normative verbal form merely because it expresses strength.

## Language rule

A stable-ID communication control in [[Ariadline Language Rules v0.1]].

## Document pattern

A stable-ID communication structure in [[Ariadline Document Patterns v0.1]]. A pattern is not a mandatory file schema, research method, theory, or rhetorical order.

## Profile

A versioned selection of exact rule IDs. A profile does not define a document genre, theory, method, scientific quality level, or terminology set.

## Profile set

The versioned register that maps profile names to exact rule IDs and applicability conditions. The current proposed set is controlled by [[Ariadline Profile Applicability Register v0.1]].

## Conformance object

The bounded text artifact or document part to which a conformance result applies.

## Conformance result

One of **conforms**, **conforms with declared waivers**, **does not conform**, or **not determined** for a stated conformance object and applicable rule set.

A conformance result does not certify linguistic truth or other substantive quality.

## Review method

The declared process and reviewer role used to check applicable controls. A review method is not a conformance level and does not imply a passing result.

## Typed evaluation record

A separately scoped record of reader comprehension, author meaning preservation, translation, accessibility, domain review, neutrality, authoring burden, or another declared evaluation type.

## Pass

A final item outcome stating that the applicable control is met.

## Fail

A final item outcome stating that the applicable control is not met and no valid exception or waiver covers it.

## Borderline

A provisional review flag for a plausible compliant and noncompliant reading. It must resolve to a final item outcome before review completion.

## Not applicable

A final item outcome stating that the rule's applicability trigger is absent.

## Justified exception

A final item outcome for a documented departure that the controlling rule or **should**-level recommendation permits and that does not mislead.

## Waiver

A recorded bounded departure from an applicable communication control. A waiver does not amend Ariadline or conceal a substantive defect.

## Extension

A declared local or publisher-controlled addition, replacement, or exclusion relative to identified Ariadline and profile-set versions.

## Not determined

A final item or conformance outcome used when scope, applicability, evidence, review process, or legitimate meaning authority is insufficient.

## Authorized meaning record

A record supplied or approved by a legitimate source author, authorized proxy, translator, community authority, or other declared meaning authority for the reviewed material.

A project-constructed meaning brief can authorize an internal synthetic test item only. It cannot establish authentic author or community intent.

## Linguistic example

Object-language material, a response, a constructed stimulus, notation, or another form used as data or illustration.

Relevant provenance is recorded through independent dimensions: source or origin, collection context, modification status, and production method.

## Attestation

Occurrence in identified data under a stated source, search, or collection condition.

## Productivity

A working communication description for extension of a pattern to eligible novel forms or contexts under a declared criterion. A project may use another declared definition. Ariadline controls the inference from attestation; it does not impose one substantive theory of productivity.

## Observation

For package communication, a directly recorded result, response, source form, system output, or other stated record. A document may declare a different theory-sensitive use of *observation*.

## Interpretation

For package communication, an analytical claim about the significance, structure, function, cause, or implication of a stated record. A document may declare a different theory-sensitive distinction.

## Evidence relation

The declared connection between support and a claim. Ariadline controls overstatement but does not define one universal hierarchy of evidence verbs.

## System behavior

Output or performance of an identified software system under stated input, version or state, and configuration conditions. System behavior is not automatically a language fact.

## Limitation

A known boundary, omission, uncertainty, counterexample, risk, or sensitivity issue that can affect interpretation of a claim or instruction.

## Counterevidence

Evidence that conflicts with, narrows, or weakens a stated claim or analysis.

## Stable identifier

An identifier whose controlling meaning is maintained across versions and is not reused for another concept.

## Canto-span

One non-authoritative project stress test and possible later adoption target. Canto-span does not define Ariadline rules, terminology, profiles, conformance, or governance.
