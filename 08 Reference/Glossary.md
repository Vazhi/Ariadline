# Glossary

## [[Simplified Linguistic English]]

A proposed controlled form of English for clear linguistic description, evidence reporting, annotation instructions, and related technical communication.

## [[Controlled Natural Language]]

A natural language with deliberate restrictions or conventions that reduce ambiguity, improve consistency, support translation, or permit machine processing.

## [[Terminology Control]]

The management of preferred terms, definitions, variants, and scope.

## [[Normative Language]]

Words with fixed functions for requirements, recommendations, permissions, capabilities, and evidence force.

## [[Claim-Evidence Matrix]]

A system that distinguishes observations, attestations, judgments, generalizations, analyses, hypotheses, negative claims, system behavior, definitions, and limitations.

## [[Conformance]]

A documented statement that a text satisfies a named SLE version, profile, termbase, and set of exceptions.

## [[SLE Profile]]

A selected set of SLE rules and terms for a document type or use case.

## [[Linguistic Example]]

A cited, elicited, constructed, or adapted form used as linguistic data or illustration, with declared metadata and analysis.

## [[Ambiguity]]

A property of an expression that permits more than one materially different interpretation in context.

## [[Attestation and Productivity]]

The distinction between occurrence in identified data and the ability of a pattern to extend to eligible novel cases.
