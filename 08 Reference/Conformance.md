# Conformance

Conformance is the documented relationship between a text and a named version of SLE.

A conformance claim must identify:

- the SLE version;
- the [[SLE Profile|profile]];
- the termbase;
- extensions;
- waived rules;
- review and checker information.

See [[Profiles and Conformance]].
