# Development Roadmap

> [!note]
> The durations are planning estimates. Evidence from each phase can change the sequence or scope.

## Phase 0 — Charter and naming

**Estimated duration:** 2 weeks

Deliverables:

- approved [[Project Charter]];
- confirmed [[Scope and Non-Goals]];
- acronym and naming review;
- initial governance group;
- public issue taxonomy.

Exit gate:

- mission, users, and non-goals are explicit.

## Phase 1 — Problem corpus and baseline

**Estimated duration:** 4–6 weeks

Deliverables:

- representative corpus of linguistic prose;
- ambiguity and terminology incident set;
- baseline comprehension tasks;
- initial [[Term Inventory]];
- initial [[Rule Inventory]].

Exit gate:

- proposed controls correspond to observed problems.

## Phase 2 — Alpha design

**Estimated duration:** 6–8 weeks

Deliverables:

- 15–20 prose rules;
- 8–12 evidence rules;
- 8–10 example rules;
- 75–125 term entries;
- draft [[Profiles and Conformance|profiles]];
- paired rewrite corpus.

Exit gate:

- every candidate rule has examples and tests.

## Phase 3 — Internal pilot

**Estimated duration:** 4–6 weeks

Deliverables:

- [[Pilot Specification Outline|Ariadline 0.1-alpha]];
- manual checklist;
- prototype checker;
- expert meaning-preservation review;
- revised test suite.

Exit gate:

- no unresolved critical precision defects.

## Phase 4 — User evaluation

**Estimated duration:** 6–10 weeks

Deliverables:

- [[Pilot Study Design|comprehension studies]];
- annotation-instruction study;
- authoring-cost study;
- qualitative feedback;
- rule-level evaluation reports.

Exit gate:

- core rules show benefit or documented operational necessity.

## Phase 5 — Beta and interoperability

**Estimated duration:** 6–8 weeks

Deliverables:

- `0.1-beta`;
- structured termbase and rule exports;
- corpus and annotation examples;
- editor integration;
- migration and waiver documentation.

Exit gate:

- conformance results are reproducible.

## Phase 6 — Public 1.0 candidate

**Estimated duration:** 4–6 weeks

Deliverables:

- release candidate;
- public review;
- resolved blocking issues;
- governance and maintenance calendar;
- complete release package.

Exit gate:

- [[Quality Metrics and Acceptance Gates|release gates]] pass.

## Post-1.0

- field-specific profiles;
- multilingual authoring research;
- publisher integration;
- formal or machine-oriented profile;
- longitudinal adoption study.
